Workshop contributions

During the group activity, participants will use a shared slide deck to capture key ideas on SA capabilities, application examples, restricting assumptions, research directions, and evaluation approaches.

Following the workshop, the organizers will synthesize these contributions into a shared online resource highlighting common and domain-specific findings, research directions, and opportunities for collaboration.

We also plan to initiate a follow-up publication based on the workshop findings and recommendations.
