Schedule

The proposed programme includes invited talks, accepted paper presentations, a panel discussion, a poster session with a coffee break, and a rotating group activity.

This schedule is only provisional. The date and time zone will be confirmed.

Group activity

Each group will be encouraged to select an application domain and envision how an SA system could be deployed for a real-world use case. Discussions will draw on, but will not be limited to, the following topics:

  1. Capabilities and design goals for SA systems.
  2. Assumptions about users, robots, and environments, and their validity in the real world.
  3. Academic and industry practices for studying, evaluating, and deploying SA.
  4. Experiences and insights from deploying SA systems across platforms such as manipulators, drones, mobile robots, and multi-robot systems.
  5. Open questions and research gaps in translating SA systems into real-world use.

Between-group reflections

Each group will briefly present its key ideas, followed by a discussion of which assumptions, capabilities, and challenges may be domain-specific and which may be relevant across domains.

Through these discussions, we will explore open questions that emerge across domains, identify opportunities for knowledge sharing and collaboration within the community, and gather insights into ways to evaluate and deploy SA systems.
