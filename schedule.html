Rotating group activity

Each group will start with a worksheet that has space for several capabilities. Worksheets will pass to the next group after each round. Each group will complete each step once, contributing to a different worksheet in each round.

  1. Identify capabilities: List capabilities considered necessary for SA systems, with an application example and a reason for each.
  2. Explore transfer: Add examples from other domains and explain whether the capabilities are also needed there.
  3. Identify assumptions: Explain which assumptions restrict the listed capabilities.
  4. Propose improvements and evaluation: Suggest ways to provide the capabilities and concrete approaches for evaluating them.

Worksheets will return to their original groups for review. Comments, disagreements, and open questions will be retained. The contributions will be made available on this website after the workshop.
