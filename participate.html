Get involved

Who can participate?

We welcome students, researchers, industry practitioners, and people with experience using SA systems, regardless of the tasks, robot platforms, or application domains they work with.

Participants will have opportunities to share practical experiences, discuss challenges, and build connections across research groups and application domains.

Submissions

We plan to invite position papers describing ongoing research, practical experiences, capability limitations, and ideas for improving SA systems. Accepted contributions will be shared through short presentations and posters.

Submission format, deadlines, selection criteria, and the submission link will be announced once confirmed.

Attendance

The workshop is planned as a half-day hybrid event, with approximately 20–40 in-person participants. An organizer will support remote participation and monitor the online discussion.

Registration details and information about online participation will be added here once available.
