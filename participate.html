Get involved

Who can participate?

We welcome students, researchers, industry practitioners, and anyone interested in shared autonomy across robot platforms and application domains. This includes those developing or using SA systems, those with lessons from successes and failures, and those considering SA as an alternative to fully manual or autonomous systems.

Participants will have opportunities to share practical experiences, discuss challenges, and build connections across research groups and application domains.

Submissions

We plan to invite short position papers sharing experiences, perspectives, ongoing work, or lessons from developing, evaluating, or deploying SA systems in real-world applications. Accepted papers will be presented as posters and made available on the workshop website.

Submission format, deadlines, selection criteria, and the submission link will be announced once confirmed.

Attendance

The workshop is planned as a half-day hybrid event, with approximately 20–40 in-person participants. An organizer will coordinate the virtual platform and digital resources to support remote participation.

Registration details and information about online participation will be added here once available.
